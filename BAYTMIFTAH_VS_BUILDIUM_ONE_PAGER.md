**One-line pitch:** *Buildium manages properties. BaytMiftah runs the entire real estate economy — marketplace, payments, transparency, and PM in one platform.*

## We win — BaytMiftah has more

These are **real differentiators** Buildium does not offer as a unified product.

### 1. Full consumer marketplace
Search, compare, save, apply, book viewings, make offers, checkout, escrow — a **two-sided market**, not just a tenant portal bolt-on.

### 2. Ghana & emerging-market stack
Mobile Money, PayAngels, GhanaPostGPS, Ghana Trust Center — built for markets Buildium does not serve.

### 3. Transparency layer
Public Event Ledger (`/explorer`) and optional property tokenization — **auditability and trust** as product features.

### 4. Operations Buildium doesn't cover
- Gate clearance kiosk (visitor QR)
- Utility sub-metering & billing
- Procurement center for estate ops
- Evidence center for disputes
- Enterprise multi-estate command center

### 5. AI & automation (broader scope)
Beyond bill-scan and comms: lease analyzer, rent yield predictor, automation builder, company knowledge hub, AI command center, predictive analytics.

### 6. Platform SaaS economics
Admin revenue control plane — transaction fees, agency plans, promo codes, marketplace commissions, verification fees. **Buildium is a tenant; BaytMiftah is also the platform operator.**

### 7. Whitelabel & multi-surface
Branded mobile apps (Company Launchpad), Electron desktop ERP (GL, procurement, bulk ops), role-specific business mobile apps.

### 8. Short-stay & host economy
Airbnb-style listings, trips, reservations — adjacent revenue stream without a second product.

### 9. Adjacent marketplaces
Mortgage, insurance, and vendor marketplaces embedded in the consumer journey.

## Competitive framing (use in decks)

### Don't say
> "We're Buildium but better."

### Do say
> "Buildium is excellent US property management software. BaytMiftah is the **operating system for real estate** — we connect **buyers, renters, owners, PMs, and the platform** in one stack, with **Ghana-native payments**, **marketplace discovery**, and **transparent transaction history**."

### Three proof points
1. **Breadth:** 70+ workspace modules vs ~12 PM pillars — marketplace, blockchain, gate ops, sub-metering, revenue admin included.
2. **Ghana moat:** MoMo + PayAngels + Trust Center — no US PM tool serves this market natively.
3. **Platform economics:** Transaction fees, agency plans, and marketplace commissions — recurring SaaS **and** take-rate revenue.

### Honest maturity line
> "Core PM is built. Payments, AI, syndication, and e-sign activate at go-live with provider credentials. Pilot-ready today; commercial launch follows config and legal sign-off."

## Quick FAQ

**Q: Do you have more features than Buildium?**  
A: **Yes, in scope** — marketplace, blockchain, Ghana stack, gate ops, platform admin, and AI breadth exceed Buildium's product surface. **No, in US accounting depth** — trust ledger, 1099, and HOA are Buildium strengths.

**Q: Are all features production-ready?**  
A: Core PM is code-complete with simulation fallbacks for payments, bank feeds, SMS, WhatsApp, MLS, and Web3. Add provider credentials only for production traffic.

**Q: Who is the competitor?**  
A: In Ghana: fragmented tools + manual ops. In US PM: Buildium, AppFolio, Rent Manager. In marketplace: Zillow, Airbnb (adjacent). BaytMiftah spans all three layers.
