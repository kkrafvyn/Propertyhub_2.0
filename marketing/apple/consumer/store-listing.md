BaytMiftah helps you discover and manage property across Ghana—from long-term rentals and purchases to short-stay bookings.

Browse listings with maps and photos. Save favorites, compare options, message hosts and agents, schedule viewings, and follow your application or booking in one consumer portal.

• Enquiries, viewings, applications, and trip status in-app  
• Documents, messages, and payments where enabled  
• Identity verification when required for offers and checkout
