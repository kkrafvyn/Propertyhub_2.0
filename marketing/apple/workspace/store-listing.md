## Description

BaytMiftah Workspace is the professional app for agencies, landlords, and estate operators on the BaytMiftah platform.

Manage org-scoped listings, leads, calendar, tasks, leases, payments, and team communications. Role-based access keeps each user on the modules your organization enables.

**Highlights**

• Publish and manage marketplace listings with compliance checks  
• Lead pipeline, contacts, and deal workflows  
• Leases, documents, and e-signature flows where enabled  
• Maintenance, inspections, and field operations  
• Ghana-focused trust, SMS, and WhatsApp integrations when configured  

**Requirements**

Internet connection, BaytMiftah account, and an **organization invite** or membership. This app is not a consumer marketplace.
