## Full description (4000 chars max)

BaytMiftah Workspace is the mobile companion for property professionals—agents, landlords, and operators on the BaytMiftah platform.

Publish and manage listings, respond to enquiries, move leads through your pipeline, coordinate viewings, and stay on top of applications and offers from one workspace tuned for the field.

**For teams**

- Organization-scoped access with role-based permissions  
- Listings, media, and compliance checks before publish  
- Leads: enquiries vs applications at a glance  
- Messaging and notifications tied to deal cases  

**Requirements**

- Internet connection  
- BaytMiftah workspace account (invited by your organization)
