# BaytMiftah Outreach Script

## Objective
Start a conversation with a target property management company, show clear business pain, and move the prospect toward a pilot or demo.

---

## Script Version 1: Direct Outreach

### Subject
Quick question: Are your property operations still running across spreadsheets and disconnected tools?

### Email body
Hi [Name],

I’m reaching out because BaytMiftah is designed to help property management teams simplify daily operations across property tracking, leasing, collections, maintenance, and reporting.

Many property businesses still depend on disconnected spreadsheets, manual follow-ups, and separate systems for key workflows. That creates friction across operations and makes it harder to stay on top of arrears, maintenance, and reporting.

BaytMiftah brings those workflows into a single operating view, so teams can manage property operations more efficiently and with better visibility.

I’d like to understand whether your team is dealing with similar issues and whether a short discussion or pilot could be useful.

Would you be open to a brief conversation next week?

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Discovery Questions

- How do you currently track property operations today?
- Where do most delays or manual issues happen in your workflow?
- How do you currently monitor collections and arrears?
- What does your maintenance workflow look like today?
- Are there reporting gaps between teams or departments?
- What would a better operational system need to do for you?
- Is there a current need to reduce manual admin work or improve reporting visibility?
- Would a limited pilot be worth evaluating?

---

## Close Script

"Based on what you’ve shared, the most relevant next step would be a targeted pilot or a short demo focused on the workflows that matter most to your team. If that sounds useful, we can define a small scope, measure the outcomes, and decide whether there is a clear commercial fit."

---

## Best Practice
Keep the outreach short, practical, and focused on operational pain rather than software features. The strongest messages are about time savings, fewer manual errors, better property visibility, and stronger operational control.
