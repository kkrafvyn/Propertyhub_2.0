# Brag Plan: BaytMiftah REOS

## What is this app?
BaytMiftah is a real-estate operating system—one workspace for listings, leasing, payments, maintenance, and portfolio operations across web, desktop, and mobile.

## The angle
Not another “property CRM.” This is the **whole operation in one picture**: editorial marketing that promises clarity, then the workspace that actually runs the portfolio—including a platform Command Center for owners.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-screen deep green. One line slams in: **“Every property.”** Beat. **“One clear picture.”**

## Key moments (the middle)
- Editorial hero energy: kicker **“One app for property operations”** over a slow Ken Burns on property photography (cream + forest palette).
- Three capability chips arrive in sequence (verbatim from product positioning): **Listings & leasing** · **Collections & ledger** · **Maintenance dispatch**.
- Simulated workspace strip: sidebar labels **Discover → Apply → Pay → Lease** with a subtle “REOS Copilot” peek—shows the app in motion, not slide deck filler.

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup **BaytMiftah** + line from meta: **“The operating system for real estate.”** CTA chip: **Get Started**.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry** — Marketing hero: “Every property. One clear picture.”
2. **Key action** — Operator opens workspace modules (listings / lease / maintenance).
3. **Result** — Platform Command Center headline: whole-platform KPIs and integration health in one place.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a serious ops platform
- Interpretation: confident pacing, editorial typography, restrained motion; let the copy breathe.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#faf8f5` (marketing), `#0d1913` (hero overlay moments)
- Accent: `#123b2a` primary, `#b64f3a` terra accent
- Text: `#14251d` on light, `#f5f1e8` on dark
- Display font: Playfair Display
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Strongest visual element: editorial hero + forest-green brand bar

## Share copy (draft)
We built BaytMiftah REOS so property teams stop juggling five tools—listings, leasing, payments, and maintenance finally share one workspace.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed with motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (when bundled asset available)
- Music treatment: fade in 0.5s, hold under UI reveals, duck 20% on final logo, fade out last 1s
- Music cue guidance: use bundled preset `cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json` — lock hero line 2 to nearest strongCue ~2–4s; chip sequence on beat grid every other beat
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — hero overlay warmth pulses with RMS on bass band only
- SFX posture: sparse — soft whoosh on scene cuts, one click on final CTA
- Audio-coupled moments: chip trio sequential; logo land on strong cue
- Restraint rule: no strobe, no dense SFX under readable text
