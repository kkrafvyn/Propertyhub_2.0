# BaytMiftah Pilot Offer Structure

## Objective
Create a structured pilot offer that helps BaytMiftah win the first real customer without permanently underpricing the business.

---

## Pilot Design Principles

1. Pilot should be short and measurable.
2. Pilot should be tied to a clear business problem.
3. Pilot should have defined success metrics.
4. Pilot should convert into a commercial contract after the period ends.
5. Pilot pricing should be temporary, not permanent.

---

## Recommended Pilot Structure

### Pilot duration
30–90 days

### Typical pilot scope
- 1 selected property portfolio or business unit
- limited number of users
- selected core workflows only
- limited support window

### Pilot target workflows
- property and unit setup
- lease tracking
- maintenance management
- collections and arrears visibility
- reporting and owner updates
- core communication workflows

---

## Pilot Offer Format

### Offer 1: Proof-of-Value Pilot
Best for a customer who wants to test the platform in a real operational setting.

Structure:
- reduced monthly fee for a defined pilot period
- limited user count or portfolio size
- access to core workflows only
- onboarding included at a reduced scope
- success metrics agreed upfront

### Offer 2: Implementation Plus Pilot Bundle
Best for a customer who needs more onboarding support.

Structure:
- reduced monthly rate
- onboarding and migration support in a negotiated package
- fixed implementation fee or discounted setup
- limited rollout scope
- defined conversion to standard pricing after pilot

### Offer 3: Strategic Pilot Partner
Best for a company that could become a strong reference account.

Structure:
- lower fee in exchange for a clear case study or testimonial
- structured onboarding support
- executive sponsorship from both sides
- defined business outcome tracking
- commercial conversion at the end of the pilot

---

## Pilot Terms to Include

- pilot start date
- pilot end date
- number of units and users included
- included modules / workflows
- support tier during pilot
- data migration and setup scope
- pricing during pilot
- conversion pricing after pilot
- success metrics
- exit and renewal terms

---

## Pilot Success Metrics

### Operational metrics
- time saved by staff
- number of duplicate or manual work steps eliminated
- lease and maintenance workflow speed
- payment collection visibility improvement

### Financial metrics
- improved collections tracking
- fewer missed payment follow-ups
- reduced manual admin effort
- reduced time spent on property operations

### Business metrics
- adoption rate across users
- number of key workflows used weekly
- degree of process standardization
- customer satisfaction with platform

---

## Pilot Conversion Structure

At the end of the pilot:
- convert to standard commercial package
- use the pilot metrics to justify the commercial pricing
- define whether the customer is moving to monthly billing or custom contract terms
- confirm support level
- confirm module bundle and onboarding scope

---

## Pilot Pricing Guidance

The pilot should not be used as a permanent low-price position.

Recommended approach:
- pilot price should be lower than standard but still above a business floor
- customer should understand that the pilot is a proof-of-value phase
- pilot should lead to a clear standard commercial package after validation

---

## Example Pilot Offer Language

"BaytMiftah will provide a limited-scope pilot for a defined period to validate operational performance against your current property management workflows. During the pilot, the customer will receive access to the core workflows needed to assess value, with standard onboarding and a reduced fee. Success metrics will be agreed in advance and a commercial plan will be presented at the end of the pilot period."

---

## Recommended First Pilot Customer Type

The best first pilot customer is a property management company with:
- 50–500 units
- several active workstreams in leasing, maintenance, and collections
- staff who can measure time savings and process improvement
- willingness to provide feedback and a testimonial

This creates an ideal path from pilot to case study to broader sales motion.
